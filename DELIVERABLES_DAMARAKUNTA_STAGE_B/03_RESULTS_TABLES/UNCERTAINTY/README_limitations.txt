Stage B final tables. Model-derived planning proxies only.
- Slope comes from a ~30 m DEM interpolated to the 10 m grid; it is not genuine 10 m terrain detail.
- Costs are ordinal model units, not INR; costs are not comparable across cost scenarios.
- PROVISIONAL choices: C1 built-up land cost = 3.0; repaired cadastral layer (_valid); equal facility weights; penalty classes PLAUSIBLE+REVIEW+UNRESOLVED_MERGE x4.
- Uncertainty experiments: 'omit' removes random scenario targets together with their footprints (missed detections); 'add' inserts synthetic translated copies of existing buildings (15-50 m) as a sensitivity test only; fixed seeds are in the run names.
- Parcel metrics are parcel units crossed, never ownership or legal access. Facility identities are unverified. TGRAC roads may be incomplete; only in-grid road cells are goals.
- No field validation, legal access, physical passability, construction savings or verified accessibility improvement is claimed.
