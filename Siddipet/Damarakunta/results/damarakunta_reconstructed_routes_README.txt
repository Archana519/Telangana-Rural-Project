Damarakunta reconstructed route alternatives

Outputs were generated from the existing raster cost surface, WorldCover, slope, road layer, and facility reference points. Existing input files were not modified.

Important limitations:
- WorldCover is 10 m and its built-up class is not a building-footprint dataset.
- Individual house roofs and facility entrances are not verified by this script.
- The alternatives are algorithmic candidates, not surveyed or construction-ready roads.
- Facility coordinates are reference records, not field-verified locations.
- Slope and land-cover inputs have their own resolution/classification limitations.
- Inspect every route against the high-resolution imagery and authoritative facility records.
